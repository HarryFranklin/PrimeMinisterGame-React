# Questionnaire changes

Every change to the pre-/post-test, by version. The version each participant
saw is saved with their answers (`questionnaire_version` in the export), so
answers can always be matched to the wording below.

## q-0.8 (18 Nov 2026, pilot) – after pilot P01

Wording only: no items added or removed, and no answer or scoring changes.

| Item | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre `lo1_2` and post `lo1_2` (higher floor) | Which society has the higher floor (lowest life satisfaction present)? | Which society has the higher floor? (The floor is the lowest life satisfaction score that anyone in the society has.) | P01 didn't understand "floor". Pre and post kept identical. |
| Pre `bi1` | …by exactly one point. Should that gain count for more if… | …by exactly one point. When a government weighs up its policies, should that gain count for more if… | P01 unsure whose point of view (the politician's or the person's). |
| Pre `gaming_frequency` | How often do you play video games? | How often do you play video games (including games on a phone or tablet)? | P01 asked what counts as a game. |
| Post `lo2_3` | Given these 3 societies, which would be classed as the better one under each approach? | Which of these 3 societies would each approach rank as the best? | P01 found the wording odd. |
| Post `lo4_4c` (Why do they diverge?) | Required | Optional | Forced answers gave junk text. |
| Post `om1_explain` | Required | Optional | Forced answers gave junk text. |
