The traditional policy frameworks (Benthamite and Rawlsian) assume that the 0-10 Life Satisfaction scale is linear. However, psychological and economic research demonstrates that human wellbeing follows a curve of **diminishing marginal utility**.

### A Shift In Perspective
The public no longer cares about raw numbers; they care about **actual happiness**. A £100 boost changes a struggling person's life, but is just pocket change to someone wealthy.
